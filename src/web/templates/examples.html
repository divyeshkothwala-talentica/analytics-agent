Tips for Better Queries
Good Query Examples:
  • Specific: "Why were deliveries delayed in Mumbai yesterday?"
  • Time-bound: "Compare performance between Q1 and Q2"
  • Focused: "Top 5 reasons for warehouse failures in August"
Avoid These:
  • Too vague: "Show me data"
  • Too broad: "Tell me everything about logistics"
  • No context: "Why failed?"
Pro Tips:
  • Include specific cities, dates, or client names when relevant
  • Use comparison words like "compare", "versus", "difference between"
  • Ask "why", "what", "how", "when" questions for deeper insights
  • Mention specific metrics like "delays", "failures", "efficiency", "ratings"
